First release.

- Block chosen apps with a tap of a paired NFC tag; tap again to unlock.
- Uninstall and settings protection while blocking.
- Daily auto-unlock time (default 10 PM).
- Lock now button, three emergency unlocks.
- Counts how often you try to open each blocked app during a block.
- Add apps to a lock while it's running (never remove), and get asked about apps installed during a lock.
- Timed days: on chosen weekdays, blocked apps open for a few minutes at a time instead of not at all.
- Phone, Clock, Settings and the home screen can never be blocked.
