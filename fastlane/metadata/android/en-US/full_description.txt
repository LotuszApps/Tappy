<p>Tappy is a no-nonsense app blocker that you switch on and off with an NFC tag. Pick the apps that distract you, tap your tag, and they're locked. Tap the same tag again to get them back.</p>

<p>Because the tag lives somewhere physical, say by the front door or in a drawer, unlocking takes a deliberate walk rather than a thumb flick.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Pair any NTAG213/215/216 sticker or keyfob. Bank and travel cards are refused, so you can't pair one by accident.</li>
<li>Choose exactly which apps to block. Phone, Clock, Settings and your home screen can never be blocked, so calls and alarms always come through.</li>
<li>Can't be uninstalled or switched off while a block is running.</li>
<li>Daily auto-unlock at a time you choose (10 PM by default), fixed when the block starts. Changing the phone's clock doesn't end it early.</li>
<li>See how many times you tried to open each blocked app during a block.</li>
<li>"Lock now" button for when you forget to tap before leaving the house.</li>
<li>Three one-time emergency unlocks in case you lose the tag.</li>
<li>Optional device-owner mode (set up over adb) for OS-level app suspension and no safe-mode bypass.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Privacy</b></p>
<p>No internet permission, no ads, no analytics, no accounts. Everything stays on your phone.</p>

<p><b>Permissions explained</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Accessibility service:</b> used only to see which app is in front so blocked apps can be closed, and to stop Tappy's own settings being changed during a block. It doesn't read or store what you type or see.</li>
<li><b>Device admin:</b> used only to prevent uninstalling during a block. No other admin powers are used.</li>
<li><b>NFC:</b> to read and pair your tag.</li>
</ul>
