# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 20 October 2026_

Tappy collects no personal data. Nothing leaves your phone.

- **No internet access.** The app doesn't request the Internet permission, so it can't send
  anything anywhere. There are no ads, analytics, crash reporters or accounts.
- **Accessibility service.** Tappy uses Android's accessibility service only to see *which app*
  is in front, so it can block the apps you chose during a block. It also checks whether
  Settings or the app installer is showing Tappy, so Tappy can't be switched off or removed
  mid-block. It does not read, store or share what you type or what's on screen.
- **Device admin.** Used only to stop Tappy being uninstalled during a block. No device-admin
  policies (such as wiping or locking the phone) are used.
- **NFC.** Tappy reads your tag's serial number to recognise it, and writes a small "open
  Tappy" record to the tag when you pair it.
- **Stored on the device only:** your list of blocked apps, the paired tag's serial number, your
  auto-unlock time and whether a block is active. Uninstalling Tappy deletes all of it.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Kjblootens/Tappy/issues.
